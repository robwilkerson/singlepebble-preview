Before You Book

Booking for Someone Else

My booking software can’t book or take payment for someone else without the appointment showing up under your name. If you’re booking for another person, please fill out the booking as the recipient, using their name, email, and phone number.

Not sure how? You can buy them a gift certificate instead, or call me at 703.869.0073 and I’ll book the session for you. Just have their first and last name, phone number, and email handy.

Cancellations

When you book, you’ll get a confirmation email with an option to add the appointment to your calendar, an email reminder 48 hours before, and a text reminder 24 hours before.

I realize things come up. However, if you cancel within 24 hours of your appointment or don’t show up, you’ll forfeit your free one-hour massage (earned on your 6th visit) the first time. Any late cancellation or no-show after that is charged in full.

No Sexual Massage

It seems absurd to have to say this in this day and age, but I’m a licensed massage therapist. That means absolutely no sexual massage.
