Meet Corinne

Every Massage Tailored to You

A Single Pebble Therapeutic Massage is an individually owned massage business specializing in Relaxation, Deep Tissue, and Prenatal/Pregnancy Massage.

Rather than having a standard routine, I take the time to listen to each client's needs and tailor the massage to meet their specific problem areas. I incorporate aromatherapy, hot stones and cupping therapy at no additional charge, per client request, on all Relaxation and Deep Tissue massages 60 minutes and over.

Please ask me about my incentives and referrals program to get free massages!

All massages are BY APPOINTMENT ONLY.
